What ICG Does

Helping You Understand the Claim — Not Just Survive It.

Insurance claims involve unfamiliar terminology, estimates, depreciation, deductibles, payment documents, deadlines and decisions. ICG was created to make that information easier to understand.

1

Understand the Language

Learn what terms such as ACV, RCV, recoverable depreciation, deductibles, supplements, ALE, appraisal and other claim terms actually mean.

2

Understand the Documents

Better understand estimates, payment summaries, coverage documents, claim correspondence and the information you may be asked to provide.

3

Understand Your Next Step

Learn what questions to ask, what records to keep, how to document your property and how to stay organized throughout the claim.

Important Scope of Service

The Insurance Claims Guide provides general educational information, organizational assistance and claim-process guidance. ICG does not act as a public adjuster, does not negotiate insurance claims on behalf of policyholders, does not determine insurance coverage, and does not provide legal advice. Questions involving coverage disputes, legal rights, claim negotiation or professional representation should be directed to an appropriately licensed professional.
