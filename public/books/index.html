Our Books

Find the Guide That Fits Your Claim.

Seven guides, one series — each written in plain language for the person holding it.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Homeowners Edition

What Homeowners Need to Know to Protect, Document, and Navigate Property Claims

The flagship guide of the series, written for homeowners who want to understand a property claim before, during, and after a loss. In plain language, it explains how to protect your home, document damage, and follow the claim process with confidence. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: New Home Buyer's Edition

What New Home Buyers Need to Know Before They Buy, Protect, and Navigate Property Claims

Written for people preparing to buy a home, this guide covers what buyers need to know before they buy — and how property claims work if damage happens after the purchase. It connects the home, the insurance policy, and the claim process in plain language. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Realtors Edition

What Realtors Need to Know, Advise, and Do When Insurance Claims Impact a Property

Written for real estate professionals, this edition explains what Realtors need to know, advise, and do when an insurance claim affects a property or a pending sale. It helps you speak clearly with clients when damage, repairs, or claim timelines become part of the transaction. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Home Renters Claims

What Renters Need to Know, Document, and Do After Damage or Loss

Written for renters, this guide explains what to know, document, and do after damage or loss in a rented home. It walks through how a renter's claim works, what records to keep, and how to stay organized from the first report to the final decision. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Roof Damage Claims

What Homeowners Need to Know, Document, and Do After Roof Damage

A focused guide for homeowners dealing with roof damage, explaining what to know, document, and do after the damage is found. It covers the inspections, photographs, and claim steps that follow a roof loss, in plain language. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Water Damage Claims

What Homeowners Need to Know, Document, and Do After Water Damage

A focused guide for homeowners dealing with water damage, explaining what to know, document, and do after a water loss. It covers the first steps to take, the records to keep, and how the claim process moves forward. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.

The Insurance Claims Guide: Rental Property Claims Edition

What Rental Property Owners Need to Know to Protect, Document, and Navigate Property Claims

Written for rental property owners, this guide explains what owners need to know to protect a rental property, document damage, and navigate a property claim. It is built for landlords who want the claim process explained clearly before a loss ever happens. Part of The Insurance Claims Guide book series.
