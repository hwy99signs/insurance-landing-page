The Insurance Claims Guide

AI & Privacy

How the ICG AI Claim Companion works, what it reads, and what it never does — in plain language.

What the AI Companion is

The ICG AI Claim Companion is an educational assistant. It answers your questions using The Insurance Claims Guide's own educational library and publicly available information from your state's Department of Insurance. It is education, not claim advice: it does not decide whether your claim is covered, what your claim is worth, or what your insurer should pay.

Your documents — only with your permission

You may choose to upload claim documents — your policy, letters from your insurer, estimates, and photos — so the Companion can help you understand and organize them. Uploading is always your choice, and it works like this:

  • Nothing is uploaded without your consent. Before any upload, you check a box giving ICG permission to read and review the document. No consent, no upload.
  • Your permission is recorded with the date, time, and the exact wording you agreed to.
  • Your documents are used for one purpose: helping you understand, organize, and act on your own claim.
  • Your documents are never sold, never shared, and never sent to your insurance company or to any adjuster, contractor, or third party.
  • AI processing: to read and explain your documents, they are processed by our AI provider's systems under contract. They are stored privately in your account, and you can delete any document — or your whole account — at any time.
  • You can withdraw permission. If you withdraw consent, we stop reviewing your documents from that point on.
Please protect yourself: never put your Social Security number, bank login, or other confidential credentials in the Companion or in any form on this site. ICG will never ask for them.

What we collect and why

We collect the information you give us — your name, email, claim details, and the documents you choose to upload — for one reason: to run your claim workspace, answer your questions, keep your roadmap and reminders on schedule, and provide the educational service you subscribed to. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your claim information for advertising.

Your state's information

State-specific answers are drawn from your state's own Department of Insurance and are labeled with the source and the date the information was last verified, so you can always check the official source yourself.

Questions about your privacy

Email us at thehomeownersclaimsguide@erantpropertyservices.com and a human will answer.

ICG provides educational information only and is not a public adjuster, law firm, or insurance company.
