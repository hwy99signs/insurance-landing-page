This is an educational consultation. The Insurance Claims Guide provides general educational information, organizational assistance and claim-process guidance. ICG does not act as a public adjuster, does not negotiate insurance claims on behalf of policyholders, does not determine insurance coverage, and does not provide legal advice. Eisenhower will not contact your insurance company for you, will not tell you what your claim is worth, and cannot guarantee any claim outcome.
